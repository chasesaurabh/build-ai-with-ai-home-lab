# Power Backup Operations

## Low Runtime Alert

A low uninterruptible-power-supply runtime estimate can reflect battery age, increased load, temperature, or a recent discharge. Record the UPS identifier, estimated runtime, load percentage, battery state, and alert time before deciding whether maintenance is needed.

## Evidence to Collect

Collect the controller status, recent power events, battery test result, and connected-load summary. Do not trigger a discharge test during an active outage merely to gather evidence.

## Shutdown Boundary

An informational assistant may describe the approved shutdown plan but cannot initiate shutdown, disable alarms, or change power-controller settings. Human approval is required before any action that interrupts protected equipment.
